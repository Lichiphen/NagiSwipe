NagiSeries

NagiSeries is a series of small tools for personal websites: NagiSwipe shows pictures large, NagiManga lets people read your manga, and NagiLog posts text, pictures, manga, video and audio. None of them needs a database or an outside service: put them on your own site and they work.

The three tools#

NagiSwipe

Image popup gallery

  • Load one JS and one CSS file, and the picture links on the page become a gallery
  • Swipe, pinch zoom, double tap, captions
  • Works with Tegalog through its admin settings alone
DocumentationDemo

NagiManga

Manga viewer and admin panel

  • Paste a single tag, and a click opens the manga viewer
  • Right to left, spreads, vertical reading (paged manga / webtoon)
  • Import from EPUB, password-protected releases, backups as one ZIP
DocumentationDemo

NagiLog

Personal log (no database)

  • Post text, pictures and manga to your own site, even from a phone
  • Video and audio: a waveform player, gapless repeat, covers and loop points
  • Categories, hashtags, search, six designs, RSS, content warnings
DocumentationDemo

Which one to use?#

You want toUseYou need
Let people zoom the pictures of your blog or Tegalog comfortably, even on phonesNagiSwipeTwo files, JS and CSS (they can be loaded from jsDelivr)
Have people read manga on your own siteNagiMangaA server with PHP 8.1 or later. It can also be put up with FTP alone, without the admin panel
Post text, art, manga, video and audio to your own site casuallyNagiLogThe same installation as NagiManga (nothing else to prepare)

How they fit together#

  1. NagiSwipe is a library that can be used on its own. NagiManga and NagiLog use it for zooming pictures too (it is included in the release ZIP).
  2. Installing NagiManga gives you the screen for managing works and the viewer for readers.
  3. NagiLog is part of NagiManga. In the same nagimanga folder, with the same login, the public site of the log runs too.

Get it#

  • NagiSwipe: just paste the jsDelivr URLs. Getting started always has the URLs of the latest version.
  • NagiManga and NagiLog: download nagimanga-vX.Y.Z.zip from the releases page and upload it to your server. The steps are in With the admin panel (PHP).
  • Source code: GitHub / GitLab (the same contents)
